## Executive Summary

CASHMATRIX is a decentralized payment gateway that simplifies fiat-to-JUSDC conversion through automated USDC swaps. Currently live on Polygon with 4 total networks supported.

**Key Value Propositions:**
- One-click fiat → crypto onramp
- Automatic USDC → JUSDC swaps via 1inch
- Multi-chain support (Polygon primary)
- Merchant-ready settlement system

---

## Problem & Solution

### Problem
Current crypto payment gateways have 5-8 step processes that deter 90% of potential users. Merchants struggle to accept crypto payments due to complexity and volatility.

### Solution
CASHMATRIX reduces this to 2 steps:
1. Buy USDC with card (Ramp Network)
2. Click "Check & Swap" (auto-converts to JUSDC)

Result: 3-minute onboarding vs 30+ minutes traditional flow.
