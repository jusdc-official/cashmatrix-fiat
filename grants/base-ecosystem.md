## Project Description

CASHMATRIX brings seamless fiat-to-crypto payments to Base, enabling anyone to buy USDC with a credit card and automatically swap to JUSDC for instant global payments.

**Why Base?**
- Coinbase integration potential
- Growing consumer adoption
- Low fees perfect for payments
- Strong builder community
