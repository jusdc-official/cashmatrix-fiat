## Quick Demo Guide (3 minutes)

### Step 1: Connect Wallet
1. Visit https://cashmatrixx.vercel.app
2. Click "Connect Wallet"
3. Choose MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet
4. Approve connection

### Step 2: Buy USDC
1. Enter amount (minimum $10)
2. Click "Buy USDC with Card"
3. Complete payment via Ramp Network
4. Wait 1-2 minutes for USDC to arrive

### Step 3: Check & Swap
1. Click "Check & Swap USDC to JUSDC" button
2. System detects your USDC balance
3. Automatic swap to JUSDC via 1inch
4. Receive JUSDC in seconds

### Step 4: Sell (Optional)
1. Enter JUSDC amount
2. Click "Sell JUSDC"
3. Instant conversion to native token
4. Funds sent to master wallet
